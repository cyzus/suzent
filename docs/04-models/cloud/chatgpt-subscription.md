# ChatGPT Subscription

Use the models included with your ChatGPT plan instead of paying for API usage.
You sign in with your ChatGPT account; no API key is involved.

## Set up

1. Open **Settings → Providers**. Under **Add a provider**, find **ChatGPT
   Subscription** and click **Sign In** on its card.
2. Open the link Suzent shows, enter the code, and approve.
3. Back in Suzent, wait until the card says it is connected, then enable the
   `chatgpt/...` models you want.

## Good to know

- This is separate from the [OpenAI](./openai.md) provider. Models from your
  plan appear as `chatgpt/...`, and API models as `openai/...`.
- Your sign-in stays on your computer and is never shown in Settings.
- If sign-in expires, disconnect and sign in again from the same card.
