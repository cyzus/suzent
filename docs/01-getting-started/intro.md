# What is Suzent?

Suzent is a **sovereign AI agent**: a personal AI agent whose identity, memory,
skills, workspace, and runtime remain under your control. Models are
replaceable, platforms are temporary, and the continuity of your agent belongs
to you.

## What "sovereign" means

A sovereign AI agent is owned and governed by its user, not by a model provider
or platform:

- **Sovereign mind.** The model is an engine, not the self. Switch from one AI
  model to another and it is still the same agent that knows your work.
- **Sovereign authority.** It acts only within permissions you set, and asks
  before doing anything you haven't allowed.
- **Sovereign vessel.** It runs on your machine, and reaches only the folders
  and devices you give it.
- **Sovereign continuity.** Its memory, skills, and settings are plain files you
  can read, edit, back up, and carry to a new computer, while your keys stay
  where they are.

People also use "sovereign AI" for nations that control their own models and
data. A sovereign agent applies the same idea to a single person. The full
definition, with a five-question test for any agent, is at
[what makes an agent sovereign](https://suzent.com/sovereign).

## What it can do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Use any model** | GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, local models through Ollama, and more. Switch any time. |
| **Remember you** | Learns your preferences and projects across conversations, stored as Markdown you own. |
| **Get things done** | Searches the web, works with your files, runs code, and drives a browser, asking first before risky steps. |
| **Work on a schedule** | Daily reports, reminders, regular check-ins, and long-running goals. |
| **Learn new skills** | Portable `SKILL.md` packages you can install, share, or write yourself. |
| **Meet you anywhere** | The desktop app, your phone, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Feishu, and WeChat. |
| **Reach your devices** | Pair other computers and phones, or connect to other agents. |

## How it fits together

```text
You ──► Suzent (on your computer)
          ├── Any AI model you choose
          ├── Memory and notebook (Markdown files)
          ├── Tools (web, files, commands, browser)
          ├── Skills
          ├── Automation (schedules, heartbeats, goals)
          └── Chat apps and paired devices
```
