Mobile app

Use your agent from your phone with the Suzent app for Android and iOS, what it can do, and how to pair and unpair it.

The Suzent app for Android and iOS is a remote control for the agent on your computer. Your memory, files, and keys stay on that computer: the phone only shows your conversations and sends your messages, under permissions you choose for each phone.

note

The mobile app is a preview. It works, but it isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet, and some desktop features are missing (see Limits).

Get the app

RequirementWhere to get it
AndroidAndroid 8 or laterDownload the latest APK and install it. Earlier versions are on GitHub Releases under mobile-v....
iOSiOS 17 or laterJoin the TestFlight beta: install Apple's TestFlight app, open the invitation, and accept it to install Suzent. Places depend on an approved build and room in the test group.

Try it without a computer

Tap Try offline demo at the bottom of the app's connection screen to look around with sample data. On a paired phone it's under Settings → About. You don't need a computer, an API key, or an internet connection: you can send messages, try a trip-planning example, approve or reject a pretend tool request, attach files, and organize chats.

Replies are scripted and nothing reaches an AI model; the pinned Welcome to Suzent conversation explains this. Attachments stay on the phone. Reset demo in Settings restores the examples, and Exit demo takes you back to the connection screen. An existing pairing is kept while you're in the demo.

Pair your phone

  1. Make your computer reachable. Open Settings → Mesh → Network access, turn it on, and restart Suzent. See Make this computer reachable.

  2. Choose what the phone may do. Open Settings → Devices → Mobile access and expand Phone permissions. Pick one of these; Full control is selected for you:

    ChoiceLets the phone
    Full controlDo everything you can do in a chat on the computer: read and organize every conversation, chat with the agent, and answer its approval requests. Best for your own phone.
    Chat onlyRead every conversation and chat with the agent, but not answer approval requests or rename, move, or delete chats.
    Read onlySee every conversation without sending anything.
    CustomChoose each permission yourself, and pick which conversations the phone can read.

    With Custom, the permissions are:

    PermissionLets the phone
    Read all conversations, including future onesSee every chat. Untick it to pick chats one by one under Conversations this phone can read.
    Create conversationsStart new chats, and branch a conversation.
    Manage conversations (pin, rename, move, delete)Organize chats, and, together with Send messages, edit and resend or retry a reply.
    Send messagesWrite to the agent, which can then use its tools under the chat's usual permission mode.
    Stop responsesStop a reply in progress.
    Approve tool requestsAnswer the agent's approval requests.

    Whatever you pick, the agent's tools still follow each chat's permission mode, and you can change or revoke the phone's access at any time.

  3. Check the address. Under Connection settings, Backend address reachable from your phone is filled in for your home network. Connections on your home network are encrypted automatically; you don't need a certificate.

  4. Click Pair a phone. In the app, tap Scan desktop QR code (or Paste an invitation instead after Copy invitation on the computer), check the permissions it shows, and tap Confirm connection. You don't need to approve anything again on the computer.

The QR code works once and expires after a few minutes; click New QR code for another. Anyone who scans it gets the permissions you picked, so don't share it.

Using it away from home

The phone talks to your computer directly; there's no Suzent cloud in between. On your home Wi-Fi that just works. To use it elsewhere, put both devices on Tailscale, or give Suzent a public HTTPS address of your own. Your computer has to be on and running Suzent; the background service keeps it running when the window is closed.

What you can do on the phone

  • Find a chat. The app opens on your conversations. SUZENT Regent and New conversation are at the top, then pinned chats, your three most recent with View all, your projects (tap one to open it, and Load more for older chats), and your scheduled tasks. Search looks through the titles of every chat the phone may read, not only the ones already loaded. Settings is in the top toolbar.
  • Talk to the agent. Send messages, watch replies stream in with their sources and an activity summary you can expand for each tool's details, and stop a reply. Long-press a message to copy or share it, edit and resend your last message, retry a reply, or branch a conversation. Edit and retry need Full control (or the manage permission under Custom).
  • Talk to Regent. If the phone may read all conversations, you can talk to Suzent Regent as on the computer, see when it's working or needs you, and open the conversations it handed work to. You can't attach files, edit, retry, or branch in Regent's chat from the phone.
  • Attach things. Tap + next to the message box and choose Camera, Photos and videos, or Files. Up to 10 items go with one message, up to 200 MB in all. They're saved in the conversation's workspace on your computer, under uploads/. If the model reads images, it sees photos directly; other files are handed to the agent to open with its tools. Attaching needs the Send messages permission.
  • Pick the model. Desktop model lists the models you've enabled on your computer.
  • Answer approvals. When the agent needs permission, Approval required appears above the message box with Allow once and Reject for each request.
  • Organize. Long-press a chat to pin, rename, move, or delete it, or swipe it to pin or delete, if the phone has Full control (or may manage conversations).
  • Follow background work. Sub-agent chats fold under the chat that started them. Scheduled tasks shows failed tasks first, then active and paused ones, with their status and next run. You can open a task's chat but not change the task from the phone.

The app is in English and Simplified Chinese, and follows your phone's light or dark mode. If the connection drops, it reconnects when you open it again and picks up a reply where it left off. If it can't, Connection help has Pair again and Forget connection.

Change or remove access

On your computer, Paired phones under Mobile access lists each phone by name with what it may do. Click Edit permissions, pick another choice (or Custom), and Save; the phone keeps working with the new permissions straight away. Revoke access cuts the phone off immediately: the next time it connects, it signs out and asks you to pair again.

Forget connection in the app only removes the connection from the phone. Also click Revoke access on the computer so its access is gone too.

Limits

The preview doesn't yet have:

  • notifications, so the phone stays quiet until you open the app;
  • voice input, or the agent's interactive forms and buttons;
  • memory, settings, or tool permissions, which stay on the computer;
  • offline access to your conversations.

For the technical design, see the mobile architecture.
