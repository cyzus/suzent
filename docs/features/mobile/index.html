THE SUZENT HANDBOOK

Mobile app

Use your agent from your phone with the Suzent app for Android and iOS, what it can do, and how to pair and unpair it.

The Suzent app for Android and iOS is a remote control for the agent on your computer. Your memory, files, and keys stay on that computer: the phone only shows your conversations and sends your messages, under permissions you choose for each phone.

note

The mobile app is a preview. It works, but it isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet, and some desktop features are missing (see Limits).

Get the app

RequirementWhere to get it

iPhone and iPadiOS 17 or laterJoin the TestFlight beta: install Apple's TestFlight app, open the invitation, and accept it to install Suzent. Places depend on an approved build and room in the test group.

The app's privacy policy is also linked from the connection screen and from Settings → About.

Try it without a computer

Tap Try offline demo at the bottom of the app's connection screen to look around with sample data. On a paired phone it's under Settings → About. You don't need a computer, an API key, or an internet connection: you can send messages, try a trip-planning example, approve or reject a pretend tool request, attach files, and organize chats.

Replies are scripted and nothing reaches an AI model; the pinned Welcome to Suzent conversation explains this. Attachments stay on the phone. Reset demo in Settings restores the examples, and Exit demo takes you back to the connection screen. An existing pairing is kept while you're in the demo.

Talk with Suzent

In a conversation, leave the message box empty and tap the waveform button to start a voice conversation. Allow microphone access when prompted. The input area shows the same animated cube as desktop, together with call status and live captions. The cube responds to your microphone and to Suzent listening, thinking, and speaking. Tap the hang-up button to end the call. During a call, the action stays Hang up (or Cancel while connecting). Outside a call, typing a message or attaching a file switches the waveform button to Send, and a running agent task shows Stop. A progress indicator replaces the action while a message is being processed. If voice needs setup or additional permissions, tapping its button explains what to change.

Redirect or queue while the agent works

You don't have to wait for a reply to finish. Type while the agent is working and the Stop button gives way to two buttons:

  • Redirect (yellow arrow) interrupts the agent and continues the same turn with your new message, like pressing Enter mid-reply on desktop. What the agent had written so far stays in the conversation.
  • Queue (clock) holds the message until the current reply ends, then sends it as the next message, like Cmd/Ctrl+Enter on desktop.

Queued messages appear above the message box. Tap Redirect now on one to send it straight away, or the ✕ to drop it. Queued messages go out in order while the conversation is open, and they are kept only on the phone until then. If one can't be sent, it returns to the message box so nothing you typed is lost. Redirect needs both Send messages and Stop responses; with only Send messages, or with attachments alone, you can still queue. Redirect sends text only, so any attachments stay in the message box.

Use a desktop version that supports mobile voice and configure a realtime voice model in Settings → Voice & audio on the desktop first. Your phone needs both Send messages and Stop responses permissions, because voice can start and cancel agent tasks. Starting a call in a new conversation also requires Create conversations. Voice is unavailable in the offline demo.

Calls end when you leave the conversation, switch desktops, or put the app in the background. Ending a call does not stop an agent task that voice already started; use Stop if you also want to cancel that task.

Pair your phone

  1. Make your computer reachable. Open Settings → Mesh → Network access, turn it on, and restart Suzent. See Make this computer reachable.

  2. Choose what the phone may do. Open Settings → Devices → Mobile access and expand Phone permissions. Pick one of these; Full control is selected for you:

    ChoiceLets the phone
    Full controlDo everything you can do in a chat on the computer: read and organize every conversation, chat with the agent, and answer its approval requests. Best for your own phone.
    Chat onlyRead every conversation and chat with the agent, but not answer approval requests or rename, move, or delete chats.
    Read onlySee every conversation without sending anything.
    CustomChoose each permission yourself, and pick which conversations the phone can read.

    With Custom, the permissions are:

    PermissionLets the phone
    Read all conversations, including future onesSee every chat. Untick it to pick chats one by one under Conversations this phone can read.
    Create conversationsStart new chats, and branch a conversation.
    Manage conversations (pin, rename, move, delete)Organize chats, and, together with Send messages, edit and resend or retry a reply.
    Send messagesWrite to the agent, which can then use its tools under the chat's usual permission mode.
    Stop responsesStop a reply in progress.
    Approve tool requestsAnswer the agent's approval requests.

    Whatever you pick, the agent's tools still follow each chat's permission mode, and you can change or revoke the phone's access at any time.

  3. Check the address. Under Connection settings, Backend address reachable from your phone is filled in for your home network. Connections on your home network are encrypted automatically; you don't need a certificate.

  4. Click Pair a phone. In the app, tap Scan desktop QR code (or Paste an invitation instead after Copy invitation on the computer), then tap Request pairing.

  5. Allow it on the computer. Once the phone scans, the QR code disappears and the computer shows which phone scanned it. Click Allow if it's yours, or Deny if it isn't. The phone shows Waiting for desktop approval until you do. You can still change Phone permissions before you allow it.

The QR code works once and expires after a few minutes; click New QR code for another. Scanning it alone gives a phone nothing until you allow it on the computer. If the QR code is already gone when you go to scan it, another device got there first: click Deny and make a new one.

Pair more than one computer

You can pair the same phone with several computers, for example your laptop and a home server. Open Settings, tap the computer at the top, and tap Pair another desktop, then scan or paste that computer's invitation as above. Pairing a computer the phone already knows replaces its old entry instead of adding a second one.

The phone is connected to one computer at a time. Its name appears under the SUZENT title, and Chats and Tasks show that computer's conversations and scheduled tasks. To switch, open Settings, tap the computer at the top, and pick another one. If it can't be reached, you stay where you are. Swipe left on a computer in that list to forget it on the phone.

Each computer keeps its own permissions for the phone. Everything under This desktop in Settings (Permissions, Enable foreground Node, Pair again, and Forget this desktop) applies to the computer you're connected to.

Using it away from home

The phone talks to your computer directly; there's no Suzent cloud in between. On your home Wi-Fi that just works. To use it elsewhere, put both devices on Tailscale, go through a relay you deploy yourself, or give Suzent a public HTTPS address of your own. Your computer has to be on and running Suzent; the background service keeps it running after you quit the desktop app.

Through your own relay

A relay lets the phone reach your computer from any network without a VPN or port forwarding. Your computer connects out to the relay and keeps that connection open; the phone sends its requests to the relay, which passes them down. Suzent has no hosted relay: you run suzent-relay yourself, for free on Cloudflare Workers or with Docker on any server. Its README walks through each option.

Everything between the phone and your computer is end-to-end encrypted. The QR code carries your computer's key, and the phone checks it before sending anything, so the relay only passes on data it can't read or change.

  1. Deploy suzent-relay and note its https:// address. Setting a registration key is recommended, so only your Suzent can use the relay.
  2. On your computer, open Settings → Devices → Relay, enter the Relay address and Registration key, and save. The status changes to Connected.
  3. Pair the phone by scanning a new QR code. The code now includes the relay.

The phone always tries your computer's direct addresses first and only uses the relay when none of them answers. A phone paired before you set up the relay learns about it the next time it connects to your computer directly.

Reset relay addresses issues new relay addresses. Use it if another Suzent took over the address, or to cut off phones and webhook callers that have the old one; pair the phone again afterwards. The same relay also gives webhook tasks an address on the internet.

What you can do on the phone

  • Find a chat. On a phone, the bar at the bottom has Chats, Tasks, and Settings. Chats opens on your conversations, with a search icon and a yellow New conversation icon at the top right and SUZENT Regent below them. Switch between Recent (pinned chats, then your conversations newest first) and Projects (tap one to open it). Load more brings in older chats. The app remembers which list you last used. Tap the search icon to open a search page that looks through the titles of every chat the phone may read, not only the ones already loaded. Going back from a chat you opened there returns to the results, and Cancel returns to Chats. On a tablet, the lists sit in a sidebar on the left, with Settings and the yellow New conversation icon in its header and search below them. On an iPad, the sidebar button next to Settings hides it so a chat can use the full width.
  • Talk to the agent. Send messages, watch replies stream in with their sources and an activity summary you can expand for each tool's details, and stop a reply, or redirect or queue a follow-up while it works. Long-press a message to copy or share it, edit and resend your last message, retry a reply, or branch a conversation. Edit and retry need Full control (or the manage permission under Custom).
  • Talk to Regent. If the phone may read all conversations, you can talk to Suzent Regent as on the computer, see when it's working or needs you, and open the conversations it handed work to. You can't attach files, edit, retry, or branch in Regent's chat from the phone.
  • Attach things. Tap + next to the message box and choose Camera, Photos and videos, or Files. Up to 10 items go with one message, up to 200 MB in all. They're saved in the conversation's workspace on your computer, under uploads/. If the model reads images, it sees photos directly; other files are handed to the agent to open with its tools. Attaching needs the Send messages permission.
  • See what the agent made. Images, videos and speech the agent creates, videos or pictures it looked at for you, and files it hands you, appear under its reply as on the computer. Tap an image to open it full screen, where you can pinch or double-tap to zoom, and touch and hold an image to save it to your photos or share it. Videos and audio play with the phone's own player. For other files, tap the download button, then share or open them. Each file is fetched from your computer the first time you see it, so it needs the computer to be reachable then, and it works through your relay too.
  • Pick the model. Desktop model lists the models you've enabled on your computer.
  • Answer approvals. When the agent needs permission, Approval required appears above the message box with Allow once and Reject for each request.
  • Organize. Long-press a chat to pin, rename, move, or delete it, or swipe it to pin or delete, if the phone has Full control (or may manage conversations).
  • Use what the agent shows. Tables, cards, buttons, forms and the other displays the agent builds for you appear right in its reply, as on the computer. Tap a button or fill in a form to answer; your choice shows as a short line in the chat and the agent carries on from it. When the agent asks you a question with options, answer it there and the reply continues. Using them needs the Send messages permission. Displays the agent pins to the computer's side panel still appear in the reply on the phone.
  • See the plan and context. In a conversation, tap the panel icon next to New conversation to open Conversation info: the goal the agent is working toward, its steps with what's done and what's next, and how full the context window is, split by what fills it. On a phone it opens as a sheet you can drag up for more; on a tablet it sits beside the chat. It's read-only and updates while the agent works. Regent's chat doesn't have it; its board stays on the computer.
  • Follow background work. Sub-agent chats fold under the chat that started them. Tasks (on an iPad, the third list beside Recent and Projects; on an Android tablet, Scheduled tasks in the list) shows failed tasks first, then active and paused ones, with their status and next run. You can open a task's chat but not change the task from the phone.

The app is in English and Simplified Chinese. Under Settings → Preferences you can choose Appearance (match the phone, light, or dark), Text size, whether to use Haptics, and the Language (on iPhone this opens iOS Settings for Suzent; on Android it needs Android 13 or later). These apply to the phone only. If the connection drops, it reconnects when you open it again and picks up a reply where it left off. If it can't, Connection help has Pair again, Forget connection, and a way to switch to each other computer you've paired.

Change or remove access

On your computer, Paired phones under Mobile access lists each phone by name with what it may do. Click Edit permissions, pick another choice (or Custom), and Save; the phone keeps working with the new permissions straight away. Revoke access cuts the phone off immediately: the next time it connects, it signs out and asks you to pair again.

Click Rename to give a phone a name you'll recognize, then Save. This helps with iPhones, which only tell the computer "iPhone". The name the phone reported still shows in small text next to the platform, and your name stays when the phone pairs again. Clear the field and save to go back to the phone's own name.

Forget this desktop in the app's Settings (or Forget connection in Connection help) removes that computer from the phone and also asks the computer to revoke the phone's access; other paired computers stay. If the computer can't be reached at that moment, click Revoke access there yourself. Pairing the same phone again replaces its old entry instead of adding a second one.

Limits

The preview doesn't yet have:

  • notifications, so the phone stays quiet until you open the app;
  • the computer's side panel for displays the agent keeps updating (they show in the reply instead);
  • memory, settings, or tool permissions, which stay on the computer;
  • offline access to your conversations.

For the technical design, see the mobile architecture.
