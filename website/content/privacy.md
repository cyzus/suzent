## How Suzent works

Suzent runs on your own computer. Your conversations, memory, files, and settings stay in the Suzent app you installed, and the publisher does not operate a service that receives them. Suzent contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

When you ask Suzent to do something, it sends the relevant information to the AI model provider and other services you have configured, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or a local model. Those providers handle it under their own terms and privacy policies.
